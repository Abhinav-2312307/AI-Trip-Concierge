# 🎤 AI Trip Concierge — 2-Minute Final-Round Hackathon Demo Script

> **Goal**: Deliver a high-energy, compelling 120-second product walkthrough demonstrating post-booking hotel continuity, real AI tool-calling, and proactive guest care.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Pitch Flow

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & The Solution
- **Action**: Open browser at `http://localhost:5173/`. Point to the top hero banner with Taj Fort Aguada booking details.
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"Judges, today booking platforms abandon the traveler the second checkout is complete. But the real loyalty, delight, and hospitality upsell happen during the actual trip.*
  > 
  > *Meet **AI Trip Concierge** — a personalized post-booking companion tailored for guests staying right here at the **Taj Fort Aguada Resort & Spa** in North Goa."*

---

### [0:25 - 0:50] Step 1: Instant Multi-Day Itinerary Generation
- **Action**: Click on **"✨ Generate My 3-Day Itinerary"** button. Watch the festive confetti and day cards render.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Instead of generic travel lists, our concierge instantly crafts a 3-day itinerary calculated from the hotel doorstep in Candolim. Notice the realistic pacing:*
  > 
  > *• Morning bastions at Fort Aguada (0.8 km)*
  > *• Lunch Balchao at Pousada by the Beach in Calangute (4.5 km)*
  > *• Evening garden dining at Gunpowder in Assagao.*
  > 
  > *Every single spot is verified in our curated Goa knowledge base — zero hallucinations."*

---

### [0:50 - 1:25] Step 2: Conversational AI Concierge with Tool-Calling
- **Action**: Click on the **AI Concierge** tab (or click the quick prompt chip *"🍽️ Dinner Near Me Tonight"*).
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Now imagine it's 7 PM and the guest asks: 'What's a good place for dinner near me tonight?'*
  > 
  > *Watch the intelligence layer work:*
  > *1. The LLM identifies the intent for dining near Candolim.*
  > *2. It fires a native tool call: `search_restaurants(near_hotel=True)`.*
  > *3. It returns **The Fisherman's Wharf** (just 2.5 km away) with exact cuisine, pricing, why to visit, and interactive reservation cards right in the chat.*
  > 
  > *We can also ask about transport, chill beaches in Ashvem, or romantic catamaran cruises."*

---

### [1:25 - 1:50] Step 3: Proactive Smart Alerts (The Game Changer)
- **Action**: Click the **"⚡ Simulate Alert"** button in the top navbar. Point to the floating warning toast and the injected chat notification.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"A true concierge doesn't just wait to be asked — it protects your trip.*
  > 
  > *When evening rain is detected in Baga after 6 PM, our concierge proactively alerts the guest, recommending indoor heritage dining at Gunpowder or Fontainhas Latin Quarter.*
  > 
  > *We also handle digital room keys, sunset countdowns at Chapora Fort, and sea swell tide advisories."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:00] Conclusion & Q&A
- **Action**: Click on **"Transport Guide"** modal to show Goa mobility guidance (GoaMiles app cabs, scooter rules).
- **Presenter**:
  > *"With AI Trip Concierge, hotels transform from a passive room provider into an indispensable 24/7 travel host. Thank you, and we'd love to take your questions!"*
